                           🌱 KrishiNet – Digital Agro Marketplace & Management System  

📌 **Overview**
KrishiNet is a farmer‑friendly digital platform designed to connect **Government Officers, Farmers, Buyers, and Agri Expert** in Bangladesh. It empowers farmers with direct market access, government support, and expert consultation, while enabling administrators to monitor and manage agricultural activities efficiently.  


🎯 Objectives  
- Digitalize agriculture management at Upazilla level.  
- Empower farmers with direct selling and advisory support.  
- Reduce middlemen and ensure fair pricing for buyers.  
- Provide real‑time monitoring and analytics for government officers.  
- Integrate veterinary and crop expertise for holistic agro support.  



 👥 Stakeholders & Roles  

**Government Officers** 
- Register and manage farmers  
- Monitor crop yield reports  
- Publish weather and advisories  
- Schedule consultations with farmers  
- Distribute subsidies and loans  
- Track fertilizer and seed distribution  

**Farmers**  
- Create and update profile  
- Report crop yield data  
- Request officer consultation  
- List and sell products  
- Participate in bidding system  
- Apply for loans/subsidies  

### Buyers  
- Browse farmer product listings  
- Place direct purchase orders  
- Participate in bidding system  
- Track orders and delivery  
- Make secure digital payments  
- Subscribe for regular supply  
- Access farmer profile details  
- Receive crop availability notifications  

### Agri Experts (Vet = Animal + Crop)  
- Provide animal health consultation  
- Diagnose livestock and crops  
- Schedule vaccination and training  
- Upload prescriptions and advisories  
- Report emergency disease outbreaks  
- Recommend fertilizer and feed usage  
- Conduct farmer workshops  
- Maintain health and soil records  
- Offer pest outbreak alerts  
- Provide seasonal crop planning advice  
- Deliver soil testing recommendations  
- Upload research‑based advisory documents  
- Respond to farmer queries directly  
- Suggest modern farming techniques  
- Maintain livestock and crop records  



## ⚙️ Core Functional Features  
- Farmer registration & profile management  
- Crop yield reporting & monitoring  
- Weather forecast & agro advisories  
- Disease outbreak alerts (crop & livestock)  
- Subsidy/loan distribution tracking  
- Consultation scheduling (officers & experts)  
- Training/workshop enrollment  
- Fertilizer/seed distribution updates  
- Product listing & direct selling  
- Bidding system for buyers  
- Digital payment integration  
- Order tracking & notifications  
- Emergency alert system (flood, cyclone, pest)  
- AI crop prediction & IoT sensor integration  
- Blockchain traceability for product origin  



## 📊 Impact  
- **For Government:** Efficient monitoring, policy execution, real‑time data.  
- **For Farmers:** Empowerment, direct market access, financial support.  
- **For Buyers:** Fair pricing, authentic products, easy access.  
- **For Experts:** Streamlined advisory delivery, better farmer engagement.  

 🚀 Vision  

KrishiNet aims to build a **complete agro digital ecosystem** in Bangladesh, connecting farmers, government, buyers, and experts under one platform to ensure sustainable agricultural growth.
